description: Build or adjust a CapCut project - tracks, transitions, effects, masks, keyframes, subtitles
---

Use the `capcut-draft` skill. Request: $ARGUMENTS

Confirm the drafts folder with `capcut-drafts` first. Search real effect names
with `capcut-effects` instead of guessing them. Build with `capcut-build --spec`,
or `capcut-from-plan` when a storyboard already exists. Tell the person to
restart CapCut to see the new draft, and that CapCut does the final export.
